Base of operations of [[The Imperium]] in the [[Primordial Forest]], being the first and only encampment of the Imperial Expedition in [[Caldera]]. 

Castrum Solis is a pretty barebones sea of tents with 3 buildings and a single well at its center. Very little infrastructure is present, but some attempts at farmland have been made and the beginnings of a wall are starting to be built.
