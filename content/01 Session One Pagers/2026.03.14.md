### In the Cursed Forest
- We begin the session the moment after defeating the corrupted plant and rock formations in the cursed forest that's one of [[Nyarlathotep's Cursed Nodules]]
- We push through the forest and exit the cursed land, entering the borderland between the nodule and the [[Mycelial Enclosure]].
### The Borderlands
- Warm and wet, rich with spores in the air, the mycelial flora was colourful. It offered a stark contrast with the dark woods we came out of.
- The mycelium seemed intelligent, trying to fight the corruption and hamper its growth. It was described as the white blood cells of the planet itself.
- There's no deific involvement in this mycelial mechanism.
- Vines and roots sprouted from both the mycelial and cursed side of the land, aiming to suck away the opposing aspect. The mycelium would actively suck away the corruption through them.
- As the mycelium is intelligent, spells aimed at communicating would allow us to speak to them. For instance, Speak with Plants.
- Falling prone while affected by either force would cause us to get grabbed, restrained and/or drained by this intelligent flora.
### Ambushed at Camp
- We set up camp, but immediately get attacked by corrupted enemies.
- One of them, a vantablack dragon. It was literally sucking the light around it away.
- Tentacled spider-like aberrations accompanied the dragon in its ambush.
- Worth noting that both the dragon and its allies spoke common, which is odd.
- With some effort, we defeated them.
### Meeting the Mycelium
* As our fight finished, a flower bloomed near us. As it opened up, a recognizable face spoke to us in very simple words. It declared intentions of an amicable arrangement where we'd feed it corrupted bodies, and they'd support us in turn.
* It also pleaded that we find a vessel for it to inhabit so that it could communicate with us properly.
* [[Zeta]] solved this by casting Speak with Plants. 
* This personification of the Mycelium started by warning us that mightier creatures were on their way to prey on us.
* It followed by requesting our help. The Mycelium was trying to eradicate the three [[Nyarlathotep's Cursed Nodules]] in order to permanently shut the corruption down.
* It hinted that vanquishing these nodules would unlock access points to the [[Mycelial Gate]].
* It offered to establish a link with us that would allow it to communicate information about the whole area. It warned, however, that the method would be invasive.
* [[Zeta]] accepted, and the Mycelium stuck 4 probes into his skin. 
* Through the link to the Mycelium, [[Zeta]] saw the [[Mycelial Gate]], a coral looking environment that led to a different dimension.
* The [[Heart of the Mycelium]] sprouted from this different dimension.
* [[Zeta]] then saw the [[Mycelial Enclosure]], a huge barrier of Mycelium that bordered the region around [[Castrum Solis]].
* It acted as a containment zone, not allowing anyone outside or inside until the threat of [[Nyarlathotep's Cursed Nodules]] was dealt with.
* The Mycelium is connected to the [[First World]].
* The Universe has two reflections: the [[First World]] and the [[Void]]. 
* The [[First World]] is still considered [[Caldera]], even if a reflection of it.
* The session ends midway through our conversation with the Mycelium. As we wrap up, it mentions that if we do not feel ready to face [[Nyarlathotep's Cursed Nodules]] yet, there are portal to other planes within the [[Mycelial Enclosure]] which we can explore in order to gather our strengths.
