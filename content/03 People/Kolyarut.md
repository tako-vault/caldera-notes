A very large cloaked construct that is shrouded in mystery. So far he has only shown himself to give the [[Shadow Wizard Money Gang]] a simple warning:

>"You are not from this timeline. You should be deleted."

And he included [[Al]], seemingly contemporary to the [[Primordial Era]], into that judgement. This opened up the possibility that [[Al]] is also in some shape or form displaced in time.
